# Quick 2D M-77 classroom test

This is a small pacing test, using the original 2D character rigs and artwork
from the earlier approved version. It covers the malfunction, three-round
encounter and maintenance record: three cue groups / 13 manual beats in one
service corridor. The previous complete drafts and saved classroom copy remain.

Movement is 520 px/s (previously 125), with quick acceleration and turning. R opens
Rhee, M opens M-77, T reveals the ticket and F instantly positions the ensemble
at the door. Matching buttons are always available without walking. NPC dialogue
uses a direct JavaScriptBridge callback, removing the prior 120 ms polling gate.
There is no typing animation or mandatory camera pan. Camera following is quick;
caption, dialogue and evidence fields retain the original story content.

The Godot scene uses original 2D sprites and cutout rigs. No 3D models or 3D
world viewport are created. The unused duplicate mirror rigs are disabled.
World drawing and animation stop behind full portrait/evidence cards. PPT photos
still take half the dialogue view, and TV fullscreen remains available. The
complete script stays on the separate GM desk. There are no student accounts,
response forms or voice pack. Academic vocabulary glosses remain optional.

Progress and the GM channel are separate from previous drafts. All M-77 fields
retain the confirmed GM bible text: TOMORROW 14:32, TECHNICIAN COHORT 01, and
Collision with unrecorded passenger resolved. Encounter counters stay [2,1,1].

This should be compared with the script/PPT on the actual classroom computer
before expanding. Automated browser timing is evidence about this test
machine, not proof of classroom engagement or physical iPhone performance.
