THE BROKEN BRIDGE • v0.6.1 • TEACHER REVIEW BUILD

Extract the ZIP, then double-click Broken-Bridge-v0.6.1.html to play.
If your browser restricts local files, open a terminal in the extracted folder and run:
  python3 -m http.server 8000 --bind 127.0.0.1
In a desktop browser open:
  http://127.0.0.1:8000/Broken-Bridge-v0.6.1.html

Keep the terminal open while playing. No Internet connection is needed.
Use the same browser and address when resuming locally saved progress.
Tap the trail or use the arrow keys. Tap landmarks to approach and interact,
or press E when nearby. Open Field log to export an anonymous JSON record.

Choose Enable sound for optional forest audio. Voice, ambience and effects have separate controls.
The language is illustrative. Textbook alignment is unverified. The English Kokoro clips and captions are bundled.
