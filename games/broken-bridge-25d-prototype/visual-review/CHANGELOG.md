# Change log

## v0.2.0 — ranger-clearing visual revision

- Replaced the v0.1.0 procedural forest, improvised figures, and line-art evidence with generated original raster art. The wet cedar shelter, post-storm canopy, riverbed, gravel and foreground ferns share one light direction and viewpoint.
- Aligned sprites, bridge endpoints, depth marker, stepping stones, and collision geometry with the new plate. Foreground ferns can overlap actors. Water highlights, window light, and foreground foliage move locally. The reduced-motion setting holds those layers still.
- Added consistent ranger and scout frames for standing, alternating walking strides, and dialogue gestures, with common foot anchors and contact shadows.
- Switched the bridge to registered damaged and repaired assets. The gap exposes the river, fitted board sections show during placement, and the full repair adds a continuous pale deck and taut rope. Ford flags and delivered supplies persist in the scene.
- Rendered the inspection and inventory art from the same prop set as the world. Compact landmark targets reveal labels contextually. Their hit areas remain 44 CSS pixels and the field log carries the existing accessible navigation.
- Kept the quest sequence, language modes, local save key, progress export, hints, and controls. Moved spawn and crossing coordinates to align with the authored assets. The build still uses one playable location and embeds all art locally.
