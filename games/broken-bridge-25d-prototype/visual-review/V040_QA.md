## Gate decisions

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| Source and rollback | **PASS.** v0.3 source tagged at `4bf774b878f1e111befe601eae24d5709f033064`. Its HTML matches the independent audit SHA-256 `ad52a8a01b9bf2f6b2ec6689303caaff154c18a468397e3300facd61771793c3`. The approved v6 references restored and the v8 audit passed. The prior 15 Lin clips compare byte-for-byte to the baseline. Approved release files were untouched. |
| Animation implementation | **PASS for authored assets and runtime evidence.** Six unique normalized frames per direction for both characters, 48 walking assets total. The running game cycles asset identities on displacement, even with scout reduced motion. Input pauses leave the actor at rest. Carry depth follows direction with a separate object. Lin's draw trace includes directional motion, deck inspection and handoff poses. |
| Visual review | **PASS for evidence capture and inspection.** Compared the running frames with the archived v0.3 images and the benchmark imagery in [v0.3 QA](V030_QA.md). V0.4 preserves terrain, lighting and framing. It adds a visibly alternating gait in place of the v0.3 pose sequence, an acted inspection and handoff, and a more identifiable physical tool path. Portrait still uses a scrolling camera and a compact belt. Fore/aft silhouettes have subtler changes than the lateral gait. Actors remain small in the desktop wide view. **Teacher visual acceptance pending.** |
| Language integrity | **PASS for the supported progression contract.** The exact elementary and middle nonsense strings fail even with correct selected sources. A matched observation and inference plus relevant learner wording are required. Tests cover equivalent measurements and relevant alternatives, conflicting and unrelated text, and the legacy path. Student wording and choices export for teacher review. Automated checks enforce route and evidence consistency. They confer no language grade. Curriculum parity against textbook, PPT, print and homework was **NOT TESTED**. |
| Gameplay, mobile and saves | **PASS in Chromium.** Eleven unit tests. Four complete network-denied journeys across both routes, both profiles, and desktop, portrait and landscape sizes. Covered wrong evidence, nonsense and route attempts; wrong socket and alignment; two-stage rope and ford orientation; guide versus direct actions; saves, reset, report, mid-carry reload, and far-bank reload. Live v0.2 and v0.3 partial bridge saves resumed in v0.4. |
| Voice | **PASS for binary integrity, decoding, normalized intelligibility and tested replay paths.** All 15 old and 7 new clips passed independent Whisper caption comparison. Actual replay exercised Lin and a field instruction; individual MP3 playback was also checked in an offline browser fixture. Human pronunciation/prosody and independent child use are **NOT TESTED**. English clips and Chinese on-screen support are local. [Instruction evidence](../audio/instructions/README.md) |
| Offline and device | **PASS for extracted HTML byte match, archive integrity, offline launch and reload in Chromium.** Browser policy blocks the managed local-file launch route; the standalone is tested via HTTP interception with requests denied. Native iPhone Safari, WeChat, and Home Screen are **NOT TESTED**. |
| Home release | **NOT TESTED.** Teacher creative approval, curriculum parity, audio suitability and target-device verification remain open. |
