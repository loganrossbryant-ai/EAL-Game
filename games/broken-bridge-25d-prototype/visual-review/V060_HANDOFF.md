## What changed

| Mechanic | Player action | Failure and recovery | Consequence and teacher evidence |

| River survey | Move the sounding carriage between the bridge approach and white stones. Lower the probe until it touches bottom and log each reading. Compare against the posted 20 cm limit for this crossing. | A dangling probe cannot return a reading. Raise it to move the carriage. Recheck a wrong comparison against both readings. | Lin can receive today’s verified sounding. Teacher export records the two readings, comparison and hint use. |
| Safety interview | Ask when Lin checked the ford, compare the dated log and current sounding, and write a correction. Elementary offers a short frame and Chinese support. | Bridge rigging | Carry and orient the two boards on the deck. Feed the rope through the inner guide, attach the far cleat, and adjust the load through the marked safe band. | A crossed or loose rope, too little load, or too much load produces a distinct visual and textual condition that the player can revise. | The repaired deck opens to visitors. Each rig trial records path, attachment, load and hint use. Guided travel alone leaves the rig incomplete. |

The dated log reads 26 cm at the white stones. The player's current readings are 38 cm at the bridge approach and 16 cm at the white stones. The marked ford is a temporary foot route for the ranger and leaves the damaged bridge closed. The repaired bridge reopens the direct trail to visitors. Each result changes the world and Lin’s instructions. The optional closing note lets the learner explain that consequence in their own words. The supplied dataset and limits are fictional scene facts.

Earlier save files with the same storage key can be imported or resumed. Their authorization and fieldwork steps follow their original edition. Fresh v0.6 sessions use the dated notice and load tests. As in earlier builds, contents of a saved route and installed materials remain, while a loose object can be collected again after reload.
