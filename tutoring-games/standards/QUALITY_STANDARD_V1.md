# Tutoring Game Quality Standard V1

Approved comparison: Allen Lesson 4 Zig game (Food, Places, Activities), **browser-tested classroom prototype**, not yet independently home-certified. The standard transfers **quality**, not the exact amount of content or the fictional story.

## Required quality dimensions

### 1. Authentic learning
- Every required activity has a named purpose tied to a real lesson objective.
- Broad exposure vocabulary is allowed; small CORE independent output is expected; STRETCH is optional.
- The target grammar and newer vocabulary appear from the opening stages to the ending, with spiraled retrieval rather than one late quiz.
- Recognize → comprehend/infer → build meaningful phrases → complete sentences → reasons/questions → connected explanation, adjusted to the learner.
- Use images, phonics, spoken models, gestures, bilingual supports and word banks as appropriate. Explanations must be semantically valid, not merely grammatically correct.
- Answer questions must be deterministically solvable; multiple valid answers are accepted when appropriate.

### 2. Immersive, polished play
- Coherent explorable settings, meaningful interactions, distinct visuals and a clear narrative purpose.
- Original approved art and sound are preserved during language revisions; no surprising black-and-white pictures, missing images, sterile quiz-wallpaper, unresponsive buttons or endless repetitive filler.
- Useful progress signals, achievable goal and finale, gentle mistake feedback and accessible retry.
- Grade-appropriate controls/text on desktop and mobile, adequate touch targets, unclipped readable English and Chinese.

### 3. Independent guidance
- Mandatory instructions are legible; when audio is necessary for independence, exact line-to-asset mappings resolve and can be replayed.
- Only approved voice replacements; no unannounced text-to-speech stand-ins or chopped concatenations.
- Learner never gets trapped past a clue without access to the needed information.
- Teacher can inspect progress and support levels without mistaking self-reported speaking for evaluated pronunciation.

### 4. Reliable distribution
- Files are real, readable and versioned; nested ZIPs and manifest have integrity; CDN/API/network dependencies are disclosed.
- Tested browser path, mobile layout and real hosted link; cold-offline service worker and real target device are checked for home-release claims.
- Non-destructive cache versions; old game and saved progress stay recoverable.

## Benchmark facts, not universal quotas
The supplied reference includes 5 worlds, 16 minigame modes, 128 tested rounds, 144 archived vocabulary items, a 54-word food/place/activity focus and 240 preserved Jesse recordings. This content amount is evidence of a substantial reference game, **not a quota**. The latest QA records flag missing new audio and physical iPhone tests.

## Explicit rejection criteria
A release fails if updated language only appears in the last world; new homework language is absent from the game without an explained reason; a correct answer is impossible or context is missing; child must read unguided unfamiliar language to advance; audio buttons are inert; existing art or functionality regresses; a 404 or untested host URL is presented as ready; or an incomplete test suite is reported as passing.
